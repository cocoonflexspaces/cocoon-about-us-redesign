New York is full of extraordinary spaces.
Most of them sit inaccessible.

We unlock them — for the brands, productions, and events that demand more than ordinary.

What we do

NYC's only fully managed marketplace for design-forward private venues.

We source, curate, and manage a private portfolio of venues that aren't available anywhere else — properties whose owners would never list publicly, in neighborhoods that define the city's creative edge.

Every booking comes with full concierge support: venue access, vendor coordination, and on-site management. You bring the vision. We make it happen.

There's a bigger reason we do this.  Read our mission →

Built by someone who has lived inside these spaces.

Cocoon was founded by Eduard Monteagudo — an interior designer who didn't just study space. He built it, lived in it, and spent years navigating every corner of New York to understand what makes a venue truly extraordinary.

01
Created 988Manhattan
Before Cocoon, Eduard created 988Manhattan — a full-floor design incubator that became the direct precursor to what Cocoon would become. It proved the model: extraordinary spaces, thoughtfully managed, unlock a category that didn't exist.
02
Lived in the first Cocoon property
As part of the deal with Cocoon's first property owner, Eduard lived in Casa Koddous — giving him direct, first-hand knowledge of what property owners need from a partner.
03
3 years nomadic in NYC
He spent three years living across New York's neighborhoods — not as a tourist, but as a designer learning every pocket of the city and the people who make it extraordinary.
04
Built to serve both sides
Because Eduard has been a tenant, a designer, and an operator, Cocoon is built with both sides in mind. Property owners are treated as long-term partners — not listings. Clients get concierge-level support from booking to breakdown.
